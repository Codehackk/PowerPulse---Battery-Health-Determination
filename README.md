# EV-BATX

## EV Battery Intelligence & Circular Lifecycle Platform

EV-BATX is a desktop-based battery intelligence platform designed to assess
the health and remaining useful life of EV batteries and determine suitable
second-life, refurbishment, or recycling pathways.

### Core Capabilities

- Battery data ingestion
- Battery diagnostics
- State of Health (SoH) estimation
- Remaining Useful Life (RUL) prediction
- Degradation analysis
- Second-life suitability assessment
- Scenario simulation
- Residual valuation
- Vendor/recycler matching
- Battery passport
- Lifecycle reporting
